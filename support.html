Common questions

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back on a new device?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same store account you bought it with.

How do I change whether ads are personalized?

Open Settings and use the Personalized Ads switch under Ads. On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking permission in the device's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Can I play with a controller?

Yes. Parsecs supports game controllers, including Bluetooth controllers paired with phones and tablets.

Where is my progress saved?

On the device you play on. Deleting the game deletes your progress, unless your device's own backup restores it.
