Travel to the farthest reaches of the universe while dodging asteroids and other surprises in this physics-based epic space adventure.

Features

  • Easy to learn, hard to master. Flick or drag anywhere to move in that direction; tap to use asteroid mines.
  • Gamepad support: aim the joysticks to fly the ship.
  • Fast-paced play that calls for quick reflexes and creates awesome close calls.
  • Upgrades to take you deeper into space: shields to survive longer and space mines to clear the way ahead.
  • Spacescapes that change every 10 parsecs, 26 planets, over 60 asteroid combinations, suns, black holes, parallax stars and… asteroid bubbles?
  • Achievements and leaderboards.
